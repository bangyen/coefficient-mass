# Suggested reviewers

These are subject-matter suggestions, not assertions of availability. Before
entering any name in a portal, the author must verify current contact details
and confirm the absence of recent collaboration, common affiliation,
supervisory relationships, personal relationships, or other conflicts.

1. **Artūras Dubickas — Vilnius University.** Direct expertise in polynomial
   height, length, prescribed roots, and reduced height; his work is central
   prior literature for the manuscript. Public institutional sources identify
   him as a senior researcher in number theory at Vilnius University.
2. **James McKee — Royal Holloway, University of London.** Works at the
   interface of number theory and combinatorics, including Mahler measure,
   short polynomial multiples, and computational aspects. Public profile:
   https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/en/persons/james-mckee/
3. **Apoorva Khare — Indian Institute of Science.** Expertise in total
   positivity and matrix positivity matches the generalized-Vandermonde and
   Chebyshev-system mechanism behind the main theorem.

An editor may prefer not to use a scholar whose work is discussed extensively
in the manuscript. The list is therefore deliberately mixed between the
polynomial-height application and the total-positivity method.
